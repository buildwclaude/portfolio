### 1. Yatri Hub — /8/

- **Card title:** Yatri Hub
- **Page title:** Yatri Hub : A bike companion app Redesign
- **Summary:** How I redesigned the rider experience to build confidence and
  reduced trip-planning uncertainty by 67% for e-bike riders
- **Meta:** Shipped · 2025 · Yatri Motorcycles
- **Page timeline:** Dec 2024 – May 2025
- **Tags:** UI/UX, UX Research, Product Design, Design Lead
- **Role:** Research facilitator, UX Researcher, UX Designer
- **Team:** 1 UI/UX Designer, 4 Software Developers, 1 Team lead
- **Tools:** Figma, FigJam, Adobe Creative Suite
- **Description:** Yatri Hub is a smart companion app for Yatri electric bike
  riders, offering real-time bike monitoring, ride history, and quick access to
  essential stats. It enhances the user experience by providing features like
  nearby charging station locations, navigation routes, and seamless
  connectivity.
- **Stated impact:** 60% increase in first-time task success by redesigning the
  homepage; 67% reduced trip-planning uncertainty; higher user confidence to
  start longer trips; increased feature engagement during testing sessions.
- **Links:** case study https://sony-thakuri.xyz/8/ · company
  https://yatrimotorcycles.com
